    Construction method and exam reminders

    Reference planes

    HP is the horizontal plane, VP is the vertical plane, and XY is their intersection. Use one projector perpendicular to XY for each point.

    Construction sequence

    1. Read the given data.
    2. Identify the required views.
    3. Construct and label FV and TV.

    First-angle convention

    After HP is rotated downwards, a point in front of VP has its TV below XY; a point behind VP has its TV above XY. A point above HP has FV above XY; below HP has FV below XY.

    Line geometry

    True length is the 3D length. FV shows true length when the line is parallel to VP; TV shows true length when it is parallel to HP. Otherwise the corresponding view is foreshortened.

    The solver validates geometric consistency and reports missing/ambiguous data rather than inventing a drawing. For inclined lines, the endpoint direction is taken as increasing height and/or distance in front of VP unless the question states otherwise. Advanced change-of-position constructions and unusual wording may need additional clarification.
