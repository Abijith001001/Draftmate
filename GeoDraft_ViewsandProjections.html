    Learn the construction

    Isometric view vs projection

    An isometric view uses true lengths along the isometric axes. An isometric projection uses an isometric scale factor of approximately 0.816 on all three axes.

    Drawing sequence

      Projection method

      Use first-angle projection: place the top view below the front view and align corresponding corners with thin construction projectors. Darken visible outlines after checking both views.

      Exam reminders

      • Use thin construction lines, then darken final visible outlines.
      • Show hidden edges dashed when required.
      • Write dimensions in millimetres and identify each view.
      • Represent circular ends as isometric ellipses, not true circles.

      This interactive page demonstrates standard solids in simple positions. It does not claim to interpret every compound object, inclined-axis case, or cut feature automatically. Follow the specific syllabus question's position and orientation conditions.
